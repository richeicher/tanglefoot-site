Your next deadline — the summer Puget Sound Dungeness crab catch record card is due to WDFW by Oct 1, 2026, and it has to be filed whether or not you caught anything, or even went out. Report it at fishhunt.dfw.wa.gov or mail the card to the address printed on it; miss the deadline and there is a $10 penalty on your next crab endorsement. Winter cards are due Feb 1.
